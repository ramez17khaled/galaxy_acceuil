Security

  • UseGalaxy.fr relying on the IFB Core Cluster infrastructure, meaning that it conforms to the same security policy.


Data policy

  • Registered Users

    Datasets/histories marked as deleted will be automatically permanently deleted after 14 days.
    An extended quota can be requested (on https://community.france-bioinformatique.fr/) for a limited time period in special cases (see default quota below).

  • Unregistered Users

    Processed data will only be accessible during one browser session, using a cookie to identify your data.
    This cookie is not used for any other purposes (e.g. tracking or analytics).
    If usegalaxy.fr service is not accessed for 90 days, the associated datasets/histories will be permanently deleted.

  • FTP Data

    Any user data uploaded to our FTP server should be imported into Galaxy as soon as possible.
    Data left in FTP folders for more than 15 days will be deleted.

  • Backup

    Be aware that no backup of your data is provided. You are responsible for exporting your important data on safe external storage servers.


Galaxy error report / Helpdesk

Note that all error reports sent from Galaxy are treated this way:

  1. It's received by a little group of administrators
  2. If a quick/evident action can fix the problem, administrators will do it and let you know
  3. Otherwise, the report is forwarded to our community support: https://community.france-bioinformatique.fr/
  4. A team of expert can then be "pinged" to address the question
  5. If an access to the whole history is required, the expert will contact you to share your history
  6. If the content of the exchange is interesting for the whole community, a post can be published after anonymization (authors, data, ...)


Limits

  • Number of parallel concurrent jobs:
    • Unregistered users: 2
    • Registered users: 25
  • Disk Quota:
    • Unregistered users: 10GB
    • Registered users: 100GB


GDPR

The information collected concerning your user account is recorded in a database by the French Institute of Bioinformatics to allow you to access your personal space on UseGalaxy.fr.

The data collected will only be communicated to the administrators of the UseGalaxy.fr platform.
The data is kept for the entire duration of the validity of your UseGalaxy.fr account.
You can access your personal data, rectify them, request their deletion or exercise your right to limit the processing of your data.

Visit cnil.fr for more information on your rights.
To exercise these rights or for any question about the processing of your data in this device, you can contact: contact-nncr-cluster@groupes.france-bioinformatique.fr.
If, after contacting us, you feel that your "Data Protection" rights have not been respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL.
