Welcome to mnhn.usegalaxy.fr



The goal of this MNHN Galaxy instance is to regroup some developments made by our teams for everyone to use. You will find here also a minimal set of tools that facilitates the building of pipelines with thoses programs. If you need a Galaxy instance with more features, we invite you to use the main ones :

  • usegalaxy.fr : French instance hosted by IFB
  • usegalaxy.eu : European instance

For MNHN users, you have direct access to the French instance usegalaxy.fr by login with your MNHN credentials with a preferential access to resources (CPU and storage).

Feel free to upload your own data to use MNHN’s tools, but be aware of our retention policy and please respect Data protection rules
